The run this skill was written from (2026-11-14). The files are `assets/example_story.md` and
`assets/example_screenplay.md` (both as delivered, after round two's fixes); the earlier versions are in
`C:/Users/sheep/Pictures/gui_chang_shi/backlog/photo_booth/drafts/`.

## The idea page it would have had

1. Everyday thing: a Korean-style four-frame photo booth with a friend, in a shopping street.
2. Device that counts: four countdowns, and a white face frame on the preview.
3. Second 0: her smiling face with a purple-nailed hand making a peace sign beside it. 「這隻手不是我朋友的」
4. Promise: 「拍攝過程的影片，我放在最後。」
5. Twist 1: 本來以為一起拍的是感冒戴口罩的小安，其實小安火車誤點才剛到樓下，相片裡右邊是空的，她一直在背後。
   Clues: wet hair on a dry night, the frame never finds her face, she arrives without footsteps or luggage.
6. Twist 2: 本來以為「已付款」是前一組沒拍就走、我賺到，其實是她自己按的，她坐在裡面等下一個人。
   Clues: the curtain was drawn, the screen never timed out, 「賺到 XD」.
7. Last shot: the empty shop and the lit screen from 0:15, with 「我想說，賺到。」 over it and footsteps on the stairs.
8. Ghost seen: frame four of the strip, her chin on the narrator's shoulder and her mouth at her ear. Shared marks:
   black jacket, wet long hair, purple nails.

## Round 1 found (all fixed in a line or two)

Story:
- The face frame and a paid screen that waits are not how real booths work → the narrator says so.
- The peace-sign hand was only mentioned when the photograph was read → it now appears during the fourth shot.
- She waited forty minutes without a message; the friend said nothing until she arrived → a LINE left unread.
- The friend's line described a hand on the shoulder; the photograph has a chin there.
- 「影片裡沒有人先出去」 had to be read twice; 「那一頁」 pointed at nothing.
- The first line gave away the ending (a rule about paid screens) → replaced by the hand.
- The reviewer also re-ranked the twists: 「she was behind me, not beside me」 is a detail of twist 1, and the paid
  screen is the real twist 2.

Screenplay:
- 3:05 to 3:10 against a 3:00 limit; the last scene, the one the opening promised, had the least time.
- 50 seconds with nothing wrong; two identical countdowns.
- The narration read each photo frame aloud before the viewer could look.
- The masked friend and the woman in the photographs looked like two ghosts.
- A black card with white text at the end instead of the shot that planted it.

## Round 2 found (a reviewer who had seen nothing; fixed without a third round)

Story:
- 「住我宿舍」 with 「出門前」 read as if the friend had already arrived.
- A phone with a dead battery that sent a LINE and took a call → a rented power bank.
- The booth's camera cannot film the booth's screen → her face lit by it, and the video cuts.
- A ghost that happens to wear a mask and stay silent, as the LINE said → the narrator sees the mask and calls the name.
- She walked in from the door but 「was waiting inside」 → the narrator looks up and she is already beside the machine.
- No luggage on a friend just off a train → the fake has empty hands, the real one drags a suitcase up the stairs.
- Realism still 3: the narrator had no life of her own → the exam week, the cold shop, what happened to the photos.

Screenplay:
- The cold open showed a corner of a strip, too small for a phone → frame four full screen with big text at second 0.
- 18 seconds of unbroken backstory narration → cut to 45 characters, the rest as a LINE message on screen.
- The second twist had no words and its plant was two and a half minutes old → the first shot again, with the
  narrator's own line over it.
- The end card sat between the last shot and the replay → removed.
- Silent holds of two seconds on the photo frames → three.

- The owner had not yet approved the story when this was written, and no still had been drawn. Whether the film
