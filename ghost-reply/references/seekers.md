## Who counts as a seeker

Someone who, in their own words, wants ghost stories now: 「求鬼故事」「有沒有好看的鬼故事」「推薦鬼故事帳號」「還有嗎」
「求續集」「想聽更多」「睡不著求鬼故事」「說點恐怖的」, a post asking 「大家推薦什麼鬼故事」, or a top comment under a ghost
post asking for more. Not a seeker: someone telling their own experience, grieving, joking 「鬼故事」 as a metaphor,
asking for horror films or games only, a giveaway, a brand account.

Skip: real deaths, accidents, crimes, trauma; the comment or post older than 3 days (Threads: prefer under 24 h); a
person or post gui.chang.shi already replied to (check the last 14 day files); comments restricted.

## The reply

- Pick up what they asked for (a place, a kind of scare, 「睡前」, 「短一點」) and recommend **one** posted 鬼唱詩 story
  that fits. Posted stories: brand/ledger.json (posted ones only) with premise/setting/mechanism in
  brand/story_tags.json (both under C:/Users/sheep/Pictures/gui_chang_shi). Only a story already posted on that
  platform (IG: its Reel or carousel; Threads: its story post).
- Speak as the account (「我們」「我們這邊」), never as a fan or a stranger who happened to like it.
- Name the handle and number: `@gui.chang.shi 第 N 首〈title〉`, plus one line of hook that leaves suspense (don't tell
  the twist). No URL, no emoji, no hashtags, at most 2 sentences, never the same line twice (check earlier day files).
- Example: 「想看山上的可以看我們 @gui.chang.shi 第 13 首〈山屋晚上的點名〉，登山隊點名，每次都多一個人。」
- If no posted story fits what they asked for, do not reply there.
