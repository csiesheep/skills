# 審稿第 5 部分：說書長篇（ghost-story-weitong）

接在 ghost-story 的 `references/review_brief.md` 第 4 部分後面，並在它的 "Return only" 那行加上「the beat positions, the joke list, the callback grades and the voice notes」。**不要把 `plan.md` 給審稿者**：它自己數，寫的人之後再比。

```
5. Long-form 說書 voice. Read <skill dir of ghost-story-weitong-v4>/references/style.md and avoid.md first.
   a. Beats. Give the character position (as a fraction of the whole text) of: the first anomaly, the ghost first
      clearly seen or identified, each twist, the biggest scare, the start of the ending. Which of style.md §2's
      mechanisms does the story use, and is any used only halfway?
   b. Jokes. List every joke in order: its position (fraction), who says it, its type (吐槽角色、旁白吐槽、自嘲、
      日常荒謬、鬼的人性反應、其他), the line in at most 15 characters. A line that is only scary or only informative
      is not a joke; a line that made you smile is.
   c. Callbacks. For each joke: A (its content comes true, becomes a clue or weapon, or the ghost answers it in its
      own words), B (same subject comes up, nothing comes true), C (no). Where does it come back?
   d. Distance. Does any joke fall within about 25 characters of the biggest scare, or in the last paragraph? Is the
      last joke before the scare at least about 270 characters (60 seconds read aloud) before it?
   e. Voice. Does it read as a person telling friends what happened over late-night snacks, or as an essay or a PTT
      post? Name the two lines that sound least like speech. Is anything on avoid.md, or close enough that a viewer
      of that channel would recognise it?
   f. At most four concrete fixes (which line, what is wrong, the replacement).
```

## 怎麼讀第 5 部分

- **審稿者的笑點數和 `plan.md` 差兩個以上**：plan 是照計畫填的，不是照稿子；先重數，再跑 `weitong_check.py`。
- **沒有任何 A 級**：寫的人想的回收沒落地。通常是兌現那句沒有重說笑點用過的詞；先改兌現那句，比改笑點便宜。
- **「像在模仿那個頻道」**：不管分數，是必改。找出是哪一句，換成我們自己的。
- **「笑點把恐怖沖淡了」**：先把最後一個笑點往前移或刪掉，不是加更多恐怖。
