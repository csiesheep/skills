# A screenplay for one short video (3 minutes or less)

For a film that goes up whole as one Reel: no 上集／下集, and its job is watch time, likes and shares. Written from
〈一中街的無人拍貼店〉 (2026-11-14, `assets/example_screenplay_short.md`), whose screenplay went through two independent
reviews before any still was drawn. The long form (第 1 部, 5 minutes and up, cut into parts) keeps SKILL.md step 0
and lessons.md; everything there about dialogue against narration, 一鏡 and the place map still holds here.

## The time budget comes before the scenes

- **Target 2:45 to 2:52, never 2:59.** Instagram cuts a Reel at 3:00, and takes and moving shots come out longer
  than the estimate. The first draft aimed at 2:55 and a reviewer's scene-by-scene count put it at 3:05 to 3:10.
- **Narration: 280 to 320 characters in all**, at about 4.2 characters a second (70 to 76 s). Character lines and
  machine or counting voices: 20 to 26 s. The rest, a third of the film, is picture and silence.
- **Every scene heading carries its seconds** (`## 5. 內．拍貼機　第三張　0:50–1:12`), and the header states the
  totals. Then check each scene: its narration characters / 4.2, plus its lines (a hoarse or whispered line is
  slower), plus the time on-screen text needs (an 18-character LINE message needs 3 s), against the scene's seconds.
  Scenes that come out tight are the ones with a silence in them, and the silence is what gets cut in the edit.
- A story of about 1,000 to 1,200 characters gives this length. The narration is not the story read aloud: it is
  the third of the story nobody says and the picture cannot show.

## The first eight seconds

- **Second 0 is the strongest picture, full frame, with the hook as big text on it.** A muted viewer has to get it
  without the voice. The first draft opened on a photo strip dropping from a slot with one corner of one frame
  visible: on a phone that corner is nothing, and the narrator's first sentence ended at second 5.
- The first narration line is 20 characters at most and is the same claim as the text (「這張拍貼裡比 YA 的手，不是我
  朋友的。」).
- **Promise the end by second 8** (「拍攝過程的影片，我放在最後。」) and renew the promise once, after the biggest
  picture (「但是影片，比相片多了十幾秒。」). The biggest picture is where the viewers who understood it leave.
- No title card of its own: the title sits on the first scene's establishing shot for two seconds.
- The cold open may show a piece of the reveal, never the ghost's face. Frame it so the crop is possible (her chin
  was on the same shoulder as the hand: hair and the frame's edge had to hide it), and say so in the scene.

## The middle

- **The first wrong thing by 0:25**, and nothing longer than about 15 seconds without a new one. Draft one had 50
  seconds (0:11 to 1:02) of setup; the reviewer called it the largest drop. Setup moves into pictures: the friend's
  cold became a LINE message on screen, and the narration lost 23 characters.
- **Let the viewer know before the heroine does.** One close-up early (the purple nails on the thumbs-up) that
  matches the cold open: from there the viewer is waiting for her to notice, which is why they stay.
- **A device that counts** (a machine's 「三、二、一」, floors, a queue number) is ready-made suspense. Each count adds
  one wrong thing; a count that adds nothing is one second of flash.
- **An absence does not film.** 「她臉上沒有框」 cannot be seen. Show the thing trying and failing (a second frame
  flickers on her face and drops), and put the narration before the beat, not after it.

## The reveal

- **One entity must look like one.** The masked friend and the grey woman in the photographs read as two ghosts
  until they shared a jacket, wet hair and the purple nails. List the shared marks in the cast entry.
- **Do not narrate what the picture is about to show.** Draft one read all four photo frames aloud. In v3 frames
  one and two are silent holds of **three** seconds each (two was too short to find her on a phone), frame three has
  one line, and frame four is silent for two seconds before any voice. That is also what makes 「你第幾格才看到她」
  a comment people can write.
- **「What she saw then」 against 「what the camera kept」 needs both pictures.** Flash half a second of the earlier
  shot, then cut to the same angle with the seat empty.
- Each still or screen that carries the reveal fills the frame. A four-frame strip shown whole gives each face a
  sliver of a 9:16 screen.

## The ending

- **The last twist needs its plant on screen again.** A line of screen text seen for three seconds at 0:15 will not
  be remembered at 2:45. Reuse the very shot, and lay the narrator's own earlier line over it (「我想說，賺到。」).
  No new narration explains it.
- **No end card.** It sits between the last shot and the replay. The brand is small text in a corner of the last
  shot, which holds four to five seconds.
- A scene after the biggest picture is nine seconds at most, opens on a sound (a suitcase dragged up the stairs) and
  ends on a question, not on an exit line.
- The camera can only show what a camera there could see. The booth's own camera cannot film the booth's screen:
  her face lit by the screen, then the cut to the earlier shot of that screen.

## Story and screenplay stay one thing

- Every clue the last twist needs is in both. Cutting 「十點半傳 LINE 沒讀」 and 「照理說會跳回首頁」 for time made
  the ending harder to follow; they came back as pictures (an unread message, the screen still lit).
- Marks added for the camera (purple nails, the suitcase, where she first stands) go back into `story.md`, and a
  number in one (倒數到三) is the same number in the other.
- End the file with each twist as one line, 「本來以為……，其實……」.

## Review before the owner

Give the screenplay with the story to a fresh subagent using ghost-story's `references/review_brief.md` (part 4 is
the screenplay: the scene-by-scene time count, the muted first three seconds, where viewers drop, whether the
reveal reads on a phone, mismatches with the story, and three 1–10 guesses for retention, likes and shares). Fix,
then a second reviewer who has seen nothing. On the example the guesses went 5/6/7 to 6/7/7 and the second round's
four fixes were applied without a third; they are guesses by a reader, not measurements. Send the owner the
screenplay, the two rounds as one table and what changed.
